# PAP-795: CRM tasks and follow-ups: task activities with due dates and assignees, snooze, recurring follow-ups, My Day view and due reminders

**Model / Effort:** Sonnet 5 (`claude-sonnet-5`) / medium — Build M

**Goal**

Deferred to v0.2 by the Execution Schedule (NJ-14 stop-loss 09-27 can reinstate). Not claimable before 10-01.

`crm_activity` has a `task` kind with `due_at` but no surface. A rep's day is a list of follow-ups; without it the pipeline is a picture, not a workflow. This adds task semantics on the existing activity table, a My Day view and reminders through the notification centre.

**Scope**

In: task semantics on `crm_activity kind task` (`assignee_user_id`, `priority`, `snoozed_until`, `recurrence jsonb`, `completed_at`); procedures `crm.tasks.create|complete|snooze|reassign|list`; `/crm/my-day` list view (PAP-169) grouped overdue, today, upcoming with keyboard shortcuts (PAP-151); task creation from deal, contact and company pages and from the record panel; reminders `crm.task.due` via PAP-136 at due time and daily digest; automations trigger `task.completed`; recurring follow-ups generated by a PAP-43 job.

Out: team workload views, calendar sync (PAP-798), project management (PM module).

**Spec**

* Completing a recurring task schedules the next occurrence; editing the recurrence affects future occurrences only.
* My Day is a saved view over `crm.activities` with a `mine` filter and relative-date buckets (PAP-279 relative dates), so it inherits sharing and defaults (PAP-172).
* Reminders are idempotent per `(task, bucket)` through PAP-136's idempotency rule; snooze cancels pending reminders.

**Interface contract**

Provides: task procedures, My Day route, notification kind `crm.task.due`, automation trigger `task.completed`, `TaskQuickAdd` component for record pages. Consumes: CRM views and detail pages (PAP-189), notifications (PAP-136), list view (PAP-169), commands (PAP-151), jobs (PAP-43), automations (PAP-174, soft).

**Definition of done**

* Unit and Playwright green; screenshots at 375, 1024, 1920 light and dark; keyboard-only completion flow.
* `docs/growth/tasks.md`; CHANGELOG.

**Test plan**

* Unit: recurrence generation, bucket assignment across timezones, reminder idempotency, snooze.
* E2E: create a follow-up from a deal, see it under Today, snooze it, complete it and see the next occurrence.

**Demo**

Reviewer opens My Day, completes two tasks with `e`, snoozes one and watches the recurring task reappear next week in the fixture clock. Under two minutes.

**Edge cases**

* Assignee leaves the tenant: tasks reassigned to the deal owner with a note.
* Due date in a past bucket after timezone change: shown overdue, never lost.

**Dependencies**

Hard: PAP-189, PAP-136. Soft: PAP-169, PAP-151, PAP-43, PAP-174, PAP-172.

**Agent**

Builder: Beacon (CRM Builder). Reviewer: Sentinel (Visual Inspector, Code Reviewer).

**Size**

M: one session.

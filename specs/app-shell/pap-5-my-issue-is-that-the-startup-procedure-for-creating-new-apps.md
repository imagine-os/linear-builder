# PAP-5: My issue is that the startup procedure for creating new apps on the fly. Is inefficient. How quickly do we get from blank screen down the layers of abstraction to the electrons flowing through the circuits???

This issue is the origin of the whole **PaperOS Core Platform** plan. Justin's question — how fast can we get from a blank screen, down through the layers of abstraction, to electrons flowing through the circuits — became the brief for a 17-project, 206-issue programme that builds the reusable foundation (universal app shell, data layer, self-hosted version control, identity, design system, quality gates, agent roster, spec builder, collaboration, realtime, multi-input, tables/views, business core, growth, migration and a vetted library set) before any individual app is built. Everything below descends from this issue.
